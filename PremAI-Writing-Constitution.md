# PremAI Writing Constitution
**The single reference Fluso (and any human) must obey before a post ships.**
Save this as a project preference in Fluso. Reference it on every draft.
Version 1.0 — built from Suhan's 9 Jun review call + PremAI positioning docs.

---

## PART 1 — THE FLOP LIST (banned patterns)
If a draft contains any of these, it is not done. Each has the fix beside it.

### 1.1 Banned sentence structures
| Banned pattern | Example (kill) | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **"Not X, it is Y"** (Suhan's #1 flag) | "This is not a policy upgrade. It is an architecture shift." | State the thing plainly: "Your data stays encrypted the whole time. We can prove it." |
| **"X, not Y"** | "This choice is pragmatic, not ideological." | Cut the disclaimer entirely. Just make the point. |
| **"The [X] that matters / nobody talks about"** | "The metric nobody talks about." | Name the metric and why it costs them money. |
| **Contradiction-then-resolve** | "The question is no longer whether the model works, it is whether the system survives." | "The model already works. The system has to survive procurement. That is the harder part." |
| **Pilot-momentum escalation** (Suhan flagged this exact rhythm) | "The pilot works. The output is useful. Momentum builds. Then the review starts." | Rewrite as one human sentence, not a four-beat build. |
| **Rhetorical-question hook** | "Where does your AI data go?" | Open with a scene or a fact: "A bank's security lead asked us one question and the room went quiet." |

### 1.2 Banned rhythm
- **Fragment-noun lists as drama:** "Briefs. Decks. Summaries. Follow-ups." / "Chat. Vision. Transcription." Use a real sentence or a proper bulleted list, not staccato fragments for effect.
- **The aphoristic closer:** ending on a punchy standalone line ("Late movers pay to close it." / "Own your intelligence.") is itself an AI tell. End on a concrete next step instead.
- **Triplet escalation:** "Security asks X. Legal asks Y. Engineering asks Z." Once across the whole calendar, never repeated.

### 1.3 Banned vocabulary (overused tics)
- **compounds / compounding** (was used 8x) — limit to ONCE across the entire calendar
- **revolutionary, game-changing, effortless, seamless, unlock, leverage (as verb), supercharge** — never
- **"the central inequality," "categorical," "structural advantage"** — once, max
- No em dashes. Ever. (Use a period or a comma.)
- No hashtags on LinkedIn or X. On Meta, max 3, and only ones with real search/follow volume.

## PART 2 — VOICE
- Plain, confident, Swiss-calm. Sound like a sharp engineer talking, not a press release.
- Lead with concrete utility. Privacy and sovereignty are the trust layer UNDERNEATH, not the headline. (Positioning doc, verbatim: "utility sells more than privacy.")
- Honest about accuracy: we give grounded output, the human keeps final judgment. Never overclaim.
- Every claim needs a proof or a link. No assertion ships naked.

---

## PART 3 — POSITIONING RULES
1. **Utility-to-trust ratio: aim 50/50.** Right now it's ~20/80 toward sovereignty. Half the posts must lead with what Fluso DOES for the reader's day.
2. **Fluso is the priority product.** At least 60% of Fluso-vs-API emphasis should be Fluso (the PLG flagship), per the company marketing brief.
3. **One buyer per post.** Tag each post: Compliance Owner / Champion / Validator / Vertical / Developer / Prosumer. Do not mix altitudes in one post.
4. **Jurisdiction claim fix (factual):** PremAI being Swiss does NOT put a customer's VPC data under Swiss law. Correct line to reuse: "You choose where it runs. Deploy in your own VPC and your data stays under your jurisdiction. Our Swiss base shapes how we engineer for regulated environments." Never say customer data "operates under Swiss law" when it runs in their VPC.
5. **Name the stack consistently:** the open verification stack is **Reticle.** Use the name, not "our open-source attestation stack."

---

## PART 4 — PROOF & CTA
- **Proof:** link to a demo, a benchmark, the Reticle repo, or an attestation viewer in any post making a technical claim.
- **CTA:** one clear ask per post, varied across the calendar (Book a 20-min review / Read the benchmark / Try Fluso free / See Reticle on GitHub). A bare "premai.io" line is not a CTA.

---

## PART 5 — PLATFORM RULES
- **LinkedIn:** hook in the FIRST line (before the "see more" fold). Under 1,500 chars. "We" = PremAI. End with one genuine question or one CTA.
- **X:** under 280 chars HARD. If longer, make it a thread and label it 1/n. No more than 4 posts on the sovereignty/own thesis across the 15; the rest carry Fluso utility, memory, agentwashing, honest-accuracy.
- **Meta:** real visual concept, not stock "person at desk." Carousel slides max 2 lines each. No rhetorical-question covers. CTA varies.

The 15 topics currently collapse into ~4 ideas. Consolidate:
- **Merge** Topics 1, 9, 10, 14 (token cost / open-source economics / sovereign infra / rent-vs-own) → ONE thesis, expressed max 3 ways total across all platforms.
